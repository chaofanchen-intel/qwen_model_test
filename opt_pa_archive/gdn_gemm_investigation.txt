RULED OUT:
1. gemm_kernel (47.5%, biggest): it is oneDNN's ngen-jit INT4 GEMM micro-kernel
   (thirdparty/onednn_gpu/.../gemm/jit, kernel_name()=="gemm_kernel"), NOT an editable
   OCL kernel. occ 40% is normal for XMX systolic (EU threads not full while XMX busy).
   Not modifiable at plugin level. The 3 biggest instances (768x8 GWS) = 225ms / 88% of gemm.

2. paged_gated_delta_net_opt (19.6%): TRUE serial SSM recurrence (S_t=g·S_{t-1}; ...),
   token-by-token dependent, cannot token-parallelize. Occupancy limiter = Work Size (53%),
   NOT SLM/barrier/registers. VBS sweep (V_BLOCK_SIZE controls v_blocks=parallelism vs
   q/k recompute redundancy):
        VBS=2 (v_blocks 64, 1024 subgroups, 2x q/k redundancy): 91.73 -> 107.02ms  +16.7%
        VBS=4 (v_blocks 32,  512 subgroups, 1x):                 91.73ms  <-- U-curve minimum
        VBS=8 (v_blocks 16,  256 subgroups, 0.5x redundancy):    91.73 ->  99.86ms  +8.9%
   => VBS=4 is the md5-lossless optimum; both directions regress. The 105ms is dominated by
      genuine recurrent compute (state 128x4 rank-1 update x ~1000 tokens), not waste.
   => chunkwise rewrite upper bound estimated ~-3-4% of total prefill, needs intra-chunk
      triangular-inverse (I - tril(beta*K*K^T))^-1, multi-day, will break byte-exact md5
      (changes fp accumulation order), high failure risk. Negative ROI -> not attempted.

3. rms_gpu_bfyx_opt (5.5%, occ 40% SLM-limited): small (~15ms), <1% of total prefill. Not worth it.

CONCLUSION: low-risk dispatch-class optimizations are exhausted. opt-4 + opt-3 (infer-TTFT
-6.5% 2B, e2e -4.4%) is the practical PA ceiling without algorithm rewrites.
